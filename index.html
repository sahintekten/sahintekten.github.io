SEDA

On a normal day SEDA handles around 300 agent runs for 100 to 160 people, with a median reply time of about 20 seconds and 99.2% accuracy on price answers in audits.

I designed how the agent behaves and when it must stay silent, built the FastAPI backend and its 267 automated tests, and run the pilot with the clinic. I also built the phone agent, which has been tested end to end with real calls ahead of its release.
