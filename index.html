SEDA, an AI receptionist for clinics

Co-founder and product lead, January 2026 to present

Clinics advertise on Instagram and Facebook, and the ads bring in more messages than staff can answer quickly. SEDA answers them, qualifies each person and books a free consultation, and hands the conversation to staff the moment they step in. It has been piloted with a cosmetic surgery clinic in Istanbul since July 2026.

I designed how the agent behaves: its prompts and knowledge base, and the safety gates that decide when it must stay silent. I also set up the testing and release discipline and led our production fixes. I built the backend in Python with FastAPI, directing much of the work through Claude Code.

I also built the phone agent, which answers calls, books and cancels appointments, and has been tested end to end with real calls ahead of its release.

FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, Retell AI, GoHighLevel and the Meta APIs.
